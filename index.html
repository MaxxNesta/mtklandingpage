[ FAQ ]

Questions,
answered.

How long does it take to go live?

Most teams run their first module within a few weeks. We migrate your opening balances, item master and customers, then add modules at your pace.

Can we start with just one module?

Yes. Start with finance or inventory and switch on the rest when you're ready. Everything shares the same ledger from day one.

Does it work across multiple branches and currencies?

MTK supports multiple companies, branches, warehouses and currencies, with consolidated reporting across all of them.

Can MTK connect to the tools we already use?

Banks, payment gateways, e-commerce stores and spreadsheets can sync through built-in connectors or the open API.

Who owns our data?

You do. Export everything at any time in standard formats, with no lock-in.
